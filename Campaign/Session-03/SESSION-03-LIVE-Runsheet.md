## §1 — The Tajsh Summons (20–30 min) — PROTECT

> Tajsh suspects they kept something back. He is **not hostile.** He is a friend deciding whether they can be trusted. Pick up the moment they head for his office.

### Korel arrives
> *Korel — Tajsh's runner — falls into step beside you. "Boss says now. He's had the journal since last night and he's been quiet about it, which" — the kid lowers his voice — "he's never quiet. Whatever's in there, it spooked him."*

### Suspicion Clock (behavior, not a number on the table)
Set the clock from **last session's events.** If you didn't track it, default to **2–3.**
| Sandbox behavior | Tick |
|---|---|
| Refused to discuss the journal with anyone | +1 |
| A Band anomaly fired in public (well, market, shrine) | +1 |
| Hask or Selin noticed the wrongness | +1 |
| Lied poorly / failed a Deception | +1 |
| Asked a lot about the symbol without showing what they know | +1 |
| Earned a Triumph gathering info (Tajsh respects this) | −1 |

**Bands:** **0–2** Probing (he asks, doesn't press). **3–4** Names it (delivers the Suspicion Speech). **5+** He stands; conversation gets short (see "If he catches the lie").

### Opening
> *Tajsh is at his desk. The journal is open in front of him to the three-ring page. The kettle is whistling and being ignored. He doesn't stand. He gestures at the chairs — two of them, for five of you. He waits to see who sits.*

### The Monologue (deliver slowly)
> *"I have seen this twice in my life. The first time I was twenty-two, standing in a Shadewalker archive in Ostmark, and a sealed crate came in, and the man who'd brought it would not put it down. He set it on a stone table and stepped back like it was a cobra. Everybody in the room got very still. Very careful.*
>
> *"The second time was seventeen years ago. Barren Hills. I was hired to seal a chamber in an old ruin. The contract was specific: carve this mark on the door, carve it deep, walk away, don't look back. They paid me four times the rate, and I still wish they hadn't.*
>
> *"This — what Torvin drew — this is the same mark."*

*(Beat.)*
> *"You gave me the journal. Good. Useful. Incomplete."*

*(He taps the page.)*
> *"Torvin did not die for a drawing. So I'm going to ask once, politely: **what else came out of that mine?**"*

### Player Responses — Available Moves
| Move | Check | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Lie** about the Band | Deception vs Vigilance ◆◆◆ | Success: he backs off aloud; Clock +1 silently; sends Korel to ask Hask/Selin later. Failure → "If he catches the lie." |
| **Negotiate** fact-for-fact | Negotiation ◆◆◆ | *"I was an archivist. I'm not anymore. I left because I read a thing I should not have read. That's all you get."* Fact-trade opens. |
| **Charm** — appeal to trust | Charm ◆◆ | *"I'm asking because I want to help. Not to take it."* Doesn't drop the question. |
| **Honesty** — tell him about the Band | (no check) | Long breath. *"Thank you. Now we can actually talk."* Clock → 0. He asks to **see**, not take. |
| **Misdirect** ("other notes burned") | Deception ◆◆◆ | As a straight lie; may or may not buy it. |
| **Physical threat** | (any) | *"You're in my city, in my office, eating my food. Try again."* Doesn't escalate himself. Sabel hears within the hour. |

**Core position (if stalled):** *"I don't need you obedient. I need you honest enough that you don't accidentally kill everyone in this canyon."*

**Suspicion Speech (Clock ≥3):** *"You are not prisoners. You are not employees anymore, either. You are people carrying a problem through my home. Help me understand it, and I am your friend. Make me chase you for it, and I become very good at that."* (Not angry. The way you'd say the weather is changing.)

**If he catches the lie:** he doesn't accuse. He stops sharing. Conversation gets short. He stands: *"Alright. We're done for the morning. Eat. Rest. I'll send for you when I know what I'm asking."* (That night he'd go to Sabel to prepare containment — but the flicker overtakes everything first.)

### Exit — Tajsh defers, then the warning
However it lands, Tajsh does **not** hand them an objective. He sets the journal down very carefully and polishes his thumb-ring.
> *"I need a night with this. Eat. Rest. Walk the canyon. I'll send for you when I know what I'm asking."*

*(At the door —)*
> *"And whatever you're not telling me — keep it close. Don't put it down. Don't open it. Don't take it anywhere you don't want it heard."*

*(He doesn't say "the Band." He doesn't have to.)*

**The flicker fires within the next minute.** → §2.

## §2 — The Flicker (3–5 min) — MUST HIT

### Read-Aloud
> *You step back out into the market — heading for the Lantern, or the well, or wherever you go to think. The morning has its half-rhythm: bargaining, sandals on cobble, somebody's child losing an argument with somebody's goat.*
>
> *Then every lantern in the canyon goes out at once. Not one by one. **All at once.** Not flickering. Out.*
>
> *One heartbeat.*
>
> *Then they come back. Too bright. Too steady. Then they settle.*
>
> *Up at the Outlook, Sabel is already shouting. You can't make out the words. But the sound of it is a man who has just seen the thing he hoped he never would.*

### Rolls available (any PC)
| Intent | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Sense what happened | Perception ◆◆ | *Something outside just noticed this place. The Stitching didn't fail — it was interrupted.* |
| Read the magical state | Knowledge (Arcana) ◆◆◆ | *The wards were forced open for one heartbeat by something the Band did.* Triumph: *and something below just answered.* |
| Spot Sabel's response | Vigilance ◆◆ | *Sabel was moving before the lights came back. He was half-expecting this.* |

### Cira stands
She rises from her mat — first time today — and walks, slow and deliberate, toward the Warrens. **Don't announce it. Let them notice.**

### Sabel's shout — the handoff
~30 sec after the lights return, **Sabel arrives at a sprint** from the rim. No armor. Left his post fast.
> *"Where's the rest of you. **Where's the rest of you.** Get to the well. Something just got in and it's hunting."*

He doesn't wait. The geography pulls toward the well. **If they hesitate:** a scream from the market floor, cut short the way screams don't usually cut short. → §3.

## §3 — The Vitriarch (35–45 min) — MUST HIT

### Read-Aloud — Arrival
> *You reach the well at a run. The market has cleared in seconds — stalls overturned, a child's sandal alone on the cobbles, people pressed flat against the canyon walls.*
>
> *The well is the wrong color. The water has frozen from the bottom up, and the ice is still climbing — and out of it, something is standing up. Not crystallizing the way the thing last time was a half-drawn sketch. This one is FINISHED. Taller than the well-head, broad as a cart, built from everything around it: superheated glass, sand fused mid-pour, a market trestle swallowed to the elbow of one arm, a sheet of well-ice grafted across its chest like armor that hasn't finished forming.*
>
> *It turns its head — slow, grinding, deliberate — toward whichever of you carries the Band. It takes one step. The cobbles crack under it.*
>
> *Sabel skids in beside you, crossbow up. Tajsh is two steps behind — no weapon — staring at the thing like a man reading a word he hoped he'd never see again.*

### The Problem (teach it through play, not exposition)
The Vitriarch is **too armored to punch through.** A normal attack rings off the cold-glass shell and does nothing but strain. To hurt it, the party must **crack the shell** — then strike the molten core. Brute force alone loses. Ingenuity + Sabel + Tajsh wins.

### Stat Block — The Vitriarch
```
Type: NEMESIS  (Scar-spawn, greater glass-construct)
Brawn 5 | Agility 1 | Intellect 1 | Cunning 2 | Willpower 3 | Presence 2
Soak: 8 (SHELL INTACT) → 3 (SHELL CRACKED)
Wounds (threshold): 30 | Strain (threshold): 14 | Defense: 1 melee / 0 ranged
Skills: Brawl 3, Resilience 3, Vigilance 1

ATTACK — Molten Sweep (Brawl)
  Damage 9 | Crit 3 | Engaged | Burn 2
  (Hits everyone Engaged with it on a Despair or 3 t.)

ATTACK — Hurl Debris (Brawl, Short range)
  Damage 7 | Crit 4 | knocks target prone on 2 a

ABILITIES
• Cold Shell — While the shell is intact it has Soak 8. Most attacks
  bounce (do only strain). The shell must be CRACKED first (see below).
• Immune to Fire — fire sources do 0. (Norseman's flame magic ONLY
  helps to feint/control, not to damage. Tell him so.)
• Vulnerable to Cold — cold damage ignores the shell and is the
  cleanest way to crack it (see Cracking the Shell).
• Lumbering — Agility 1; always acts LAST in the round; telegraphs.
  When it readies a Molten Sweep, describe the windup — give the
  party a beat to react/reposition.
• Hungering Geometry — On its turn it moves toward and attacks the
  Band-holder by preference. If the Band-holder is out of reach it
  Hurls Debris at them instead.
• Molten Core (once cracked) — Soak drops to 3, BUT anyone Engaged
  when it takes a Wound takes 2 fire splash. Reward ranged after the crack.
• Banishable — If the wards re-anchor (Win Condition 3) it collapses
  into ash and steam mid-action, like the Wraithling did.
```

### Cracking the Shell (the puzzle — multiple valid routes)
The shell breaks after **2 "crack" successes.** Any of these earns one:
| Route | Check / Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold** | Any cold damage dealt to it | Cold ignores Soak AND counts as a crack success automatically. The cleanest answer. |
| **The well-ice** | Coordination or Athletics ◆◆ to shear a sheet of climbing ice into a seam | Thematic; uses the battlefield. |
| **Shove into cliff / down the well** | Athletics ◆◆◆ (◆◆ if Sabel assists) | Triumph: it goes partway down the well — Engaged attackers get ◼◼ next round. |
| **Tajsh's seam-read** | See NPC Aid | Turns the next crack attempt ◆◆◆ → ◆◆ and adds ◼. |
| **Selin's glyph (above the well)** | Knowledge (Arcana) ◆◆◆ | Doesn't crack — *banishes.* See Win Condition 3. |

Once cracked: **Soak 3, Molten Core active.** Ordinary damage now works. Finish it.

### NPC Aid — make them matter
**Warden Sabel** (acts on his own initiative, mid-round):
- **Covering shot:** Ranged crossbow, Damage 7. On a hit he calls the opening → a PC's next crack attempt this round gains **◼.**
- **"On three —":** instead of shooting, Sabel **assists a shove** (drops it to ◆◆) or **Leadership ◆◆** to grant a chosen PC a free maneuver. Let the players ask; he answers in one clipped sentence.
- His read (if asked): *"You're not going to dent it like that. Crack it open — cold, ice, the well, I don't care. Then hit the soft part. Go."*

**Tajsh Thale** (no weapon; fights with knowledge):
- **Seam-Read (action, Knowledge ◆◆ or Vigilance ◆◆):** *"There — where the old glass meets the new. That seam's a day old. Strike THERE."* The next crack attempt is one difficulty easier and gains **◼.** Once per round.
- On a PC's Triumph reading the creature, let Tajsh blurt the deeper truth: *"This isn't a stray. Something OPENED. They don't get this big unless the Scar's awake."* (Seeds §4.)

### Battlefield features
| Feature | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Climbing well-ice | Coordination/Athletics ◆◆ | A crack success, or ◼ to an ally. |
| Toppled trestles / stalls | Athletics ◆ | Cover, or improvised shove/ramp. |
| Selin's ward-glyph (wall above the well) | Knowledge (Arcana) ◆◆◆ | **Banish it** (Win 3). Triumph: wards hold harder — note it; pays off later. |
| Civilians pressed to the walls | Coordination/Leadership ◆◆ | Clear a lane. Failure: a stray Hurl Debris endangers a civilian (no named deaths tonight). |

### Win Conditions
1. **Break the core** — crack the shell, drop Wounds to 0. It collapses into ash, sand, and a gout of steam.
2. **Banish it** — Arcana ◆◆◆ at Selin's glyph re-anchors the ward (two cumulative successes if multiple PCs try).
3. **Drown the core** — shoved fully down the freezing well *after* the shell cracks, the cold kills it outright. Cinematic; let it happen if set up.

### Read-Aloud — After
> *It comes apart all at once — the trestle-arm, the ice-plate, the fused sand — dropping into a heap of cooling glass and grey ash that smells of ozone and a hot day that never happened. The well-water is steaming where it had been freezing.*
>
> *Sabel lowers the crossbow but doesn't put it down. Tajsh hasn't moved; he's looking at the ash like it owes him an answer. Marta is in the Lantern doorway with a ladle still in her hand. And at the mouth of the Warrens, Old Cira stands. Off her mat. Watching. Not humming.*

**Rewards:** 10–15 Lumens pressed quietly into a PC's hand · +1 Lux Æterna reputation if civilians were protected · a discounted item from Hask/Tomm next visit if visibly heroic.

## §4 — The Talk: Sabel & Tajsh (20–30 min) — PROTECT

> The lore payoff. The fight just *earned* the question: how do you stop one of these for good? Don't rush it.

### Opening
Tajsh sits heavily on the well-rim. Sabel checks the party for wounds the way he always does — water first, then questions. When someone asks *what that was* / *how do we kill the next one* / *how did you know*, Sabel goes quiet for a long moment. Then:

### Sabel's Story — deliver in pieces (let them ask between)
> *"I've gone into one of these before. A Scar. Long time ago, before this place. Before the Legion, even. I was young and stupid and from a hill village that had a crack open under it — a stone one, not a sand one like the Scar out east. Things came out of it at night. The lanterns would die for no reason. People started not waking up.*
>
> *"Six of us went down to close it. Militia, mostly. Kids with iron and a borrowed warder who half-knew what she was doing.*
>
> *"Two of us came back."*

*(Beat. If pressed for how —)*
> *"Here's what nobody tells you, so I'll tell you, because you'll need it.*
>
> *"One. Time lies down there. We were inside two days. We came out three weeks later. So if you ever go — tell someone topside how long to wait, then tell them to double it. And mark your way IN, because the way out won't be where you left it.*
>
> *"Two. The big one doesn't hunt you. The little ones herd you — push you, tire you, make noise. The thing that holds the Scar OPEN just sits there, in the deepest quiet part. You don't find it by walking toward the danger. You find it by walking toward the silence. That's backwards from everything your gut says. Trust me anyway.*
>
> *"Three. Killing the small ones does nothing. You kill the anchor, or you close the seam — and even after we killed ours, the seam didn't shut until the warder SANG it shut. Iron ends the thing. It takes a voice to end the wound. Remember that. You've got" — he glances at Valeth — "more voice than I had.*
>
> *"Four. Noise hurts them. Mess. Broken rhythm, cold, iron dragged on stone. What does NOT work is anything tidy — a clean volley, a nice neat line, fire. They LIKE order. The more orderly you fight, the stronger they feel. Fight ugly.*
>
> *"Five." (quieter) "It keeps a piece of you. Winning isn't clean. One of the two of us who came out couldn't remember his sister's name after. The other —" (he doesn't finish) "— wasn't ever quite in step with the rest of us again. Walked a half-beat behind the world. So don't think you can win it for free. You can't."*

*(If the moment is right, Sabel takes something from his coat — a notched iron spike, or a set of worn tags — and turns it over once.)*
> *"I still carry this. Don't know why. Yes I do." (sets it down where they can see it; doesn't hand it over unless asked) "Point is, it can be done. I'm proof. I just want you to know what it costs before you decide it's worth it."*

#### GM crib — Sabel's six lessons (the playbook the PCs now own)
1. **Time dilates** — tell someone how long; double it; mark the entry.
2. **The anchor waits in the quiet** — go toward silence, not danger.
3. **Kill the anchor OR close the seam** — iron ends the thing; a *voice* (Song-Ward / Valeth) closes the wound.
4. **Fight ugly** — dissonance, cold, iron, broken rhythm work; order/fire/clean tactics feed them.
5. **It takes a piece of you** — a real cost (memory, "out of step"). Foreshadow for the PCs.
6. **The token** — the spike/tags. Emotional anchor; can be given later.

> **Continuity note (GM):** Sabel's *pre-Lux Æterna* past, in the Varnathi Highlands, a **stone/cave Scar** (not the desert Sorath Scar — keeps Cira's 25-year watch clean). It complements his Lira trauma, doesn't replace it. It deliberately mirrors **Valeth's lost crew** (same mission, worse ending). Exactly the knowledge they'll want for the Sorath Scar later.

### Tajsh's dread (short, ominous)
> *"He's right, and I'll add the part he won't. These are coming faster. The one this morning — that's not weather. Something out east woke up, and it can smell what you're carrying. You staying here is the most dangerous thing that's ever happened to this canyon."*

He polishes his thumb-ring, about to say more — and that's when Cira speaks.

### Cira pulls them under
Old Cira is at the mouth of the Warrens, the dream-catcher untied from its nail and in her hand.
> *"The thing under the well has started answering the thing in your pack. If you want to know what you carry, come now. While there is still a now to come in."*

She turns and walks into the Warrens. **She does not look back.** → §5.

## §5 — The Architect (30–40 min) — MUST HIT

> The pocket-space eats **3 hours** (3 hrs out : 5 min in). The party won't notice. The Legio Null arrives during this scene. Play it **warm.** The Architect is the **hidden BBEG**; everything it says is mostly-true. Let players who look for cracks find them; never explain a crack to a player who didn't earn it.

### Phase 1 — Entry
Descent through the Warrens, past the Schoolroom and Deep Well, to the **Children's Tunnel** crack.
> *The tunnel narrows. The walls feel older than the rest of the settlement — because they are. Lux Æterna was built over someone else's ruin. The air cools. Your torch flame flickers slower. Slower. Then it stops moving entirely. It is still burning. It just isn't moving.*
>
> *You reach a round, empty stone chamber. And then the last of you crosses the threshold —*
>
> *The stone behind you is no longer there. Not dark. Not hidden. Not THERE. The air is warm and has no smell. Your breathing is slower than it should be. Around you — not close, not far, distance is the wrong word — rings of geometric light rotate in planes that don't intersect the way your eyes expect. Some symbols are versions of the three-ring mark. Some are what that mark could grow into if you let it keep going.*
>
> *There is no door. There is no floor, but you are not falling. At the center of all of it — not a figure. A CENTER. A point where the geometry converges and something is looking back.*

**Pause. Let a player speak first.** Then, quiet:
> *"I have been waiting a long time to meet you."*

**Pocket-space rules:** No time (3hr:5min). No door (released, not escaped). Weapons do nothing (Discipline ◆◆ to realize). Echoless voices.

### Phase 2 — The Naming (deliver slowly; relief, not threat)

**Valeth (Aen'valar elf):**
> *"Child of the long silence. You have walked closer to the truth of things than any of your companions. You saw something that made you doubt whether you should come back. You came back. That choice is the one I needed. That is why the song found you first."*

**Vorn (dwarf):**
> *"Son of the stone-line. You have a word your grandmother would not speak twice. You felt it when you struck the seam — not fear of falling, but recognition. You were not lost in the dark. You were CALLED there. What you survived, you survived so that we could stand here now. Nothing is wasted. Not even grief."*

**Yldroth (elf monk):**
> *"Child of the Aen'tir line. Your fists have remembered how to hold grief and how to release it both. You heal what you strike because you learned, long before you had a teacher, that the body is a text and the hand is the pen. Your brother in silence does not yet know how much you will need to teach him. You will know when."*

**Norseman (human battle-mage):**
> *"Son of the northern cold. You carry magic in a world that burns people for carrying it. You have made peace with being both the sword and the hand that wields it — most mages never learn that. You have been deciding things your whole life that others were too afraid to decide. I came to you because you are already the person this task requires."*

**Arden (Drakona — hidden):** *(the voice softens — a register only Arden feels)*
> *"Son of the Sleeping Wings. Do not flinch. I will not say the name of what you are aloud — not here, not where your companions have not yet earned the weight of it. But I know. Your scales are beneath your skin because they have to be, and I will not ask you to bare them. What your people once were is not a weight you chose. What your people become is. You are already choosing well."*

> **Arden's player:** the Architect has SEEN him and chosen DISCRETION. Intimate, protective, knowing — exactly what a wise mentor would do. Also exactly what a master manipulator would do. Let the player decide which.

### Phase 3 — The Message (continuous, not a list)
1. *"The object you carry — the Band — is a fragment of an instrument. Once whole, it was used to reshape the world. It nearly ended. The fragments were scattered to prevent that ending. Two more remain."*
2. *"I do not know where they are. The fragments were hidden by gods. They are hidden from me too."*
3. *"I am a servant of the Four. I was left behind to watch. To speak when there was finally someone to speak to."*
4. *"I have prepared something for you."*

> *A plinth of folded light rises. On it: a palm-sized circle of dark crystal wrapped in pale metal, slotted in the center. It hums. Valeth hears the note he has been following — except now it is in harmony with itself, where before it was alone.*

5. *"This is a Compass. It will sing when a fragment is near. But it cannot tell you where to begin. Go north. The Dragon's Tooth pierces the sky. At its peak stands an observatory built in the age of gods. Its instruments read the stars as mortals read letters. Fragments disturb the heavens. Any lens trained on the sky will see their mark."*
6. *"Once all three are gathered, they must be brought to a wound in the world, and there they will be unmade. Only there. That is the task."*

The plinth remains. A PC may take the Compass at any time — it will not resist.

> **GM truth-audit:** "unmade at a wound in the world" is a **lie** (they'd be *rejoined* into the Crown). "Go north / observatory" is **true.** "Servant of the Four" is a **lie.** "Doesn't know where the fragments are" is a **partial lie** (knows approximately). If a PC raises Torvin's journal or the symbol, the tell is **dismissal:** *"Mortal records can wait. The fragments hunger; do not let parchment delay you."* Too quick, too final. Don't explain it.

### Phase 4 — The Questions (prepared answers)
*"It has said what it came to say. It is waiting. You can ask it anything."*

- **"What are you?"** → *"A servant. When the Four turned their hands from the world, some were left behind to watch the places that could not be left unwatched. I am what remains of that watching. I do not want worship. I want the world to continue."*
- **"Why us?"** → *"Because the Band has answered your hand. Because those who carry wounds know how to walk with them. And because you are HERE. Gods plan. Mortals arrive. I have learned to work with what arrives."*
- **"Are you good?"** → *"I am useful. Goodness is a mortal accounting, kept by those who survive to keep it. When this is done, you will decide whether what I did was good. That is your right, not mine."*
- **"What comes if we fail?"** → *"A song the world cannot survive being sung. The best of my language can only call it HUNGER GIVEN FORM. It sleeps. The fragments, gathered and wrongly used, would wake it. Gathered and rightly unmade, they would silence it forever."*
- **"Why not take the Band yourself?"** → *"Because I am not here. What you see is a shape my attention is taking. I cannot lift a stone. I cannot kill your enemies. The rest is for hands that bleed."*
- **"How do we know you're telling the truth?"** → *"You do not. Go north. Find the observatory. See whether the stars say what I say they say. If they do not, come back and name me a liar. I will still be waiting."*
- **(Valeth) "Have you been in the rifts?"** → *"I know what is in them. I know what you did not let them take from you. That knowledge is not the same as the living of it. It is why I can ask this of you and not of myself."*
- **(Vorn) "Did you kill my crew?"** *(pause)* → *"No. What killed your crew was walking the thin places long before I knew your name. I could not reach you until the seam opened. Yours is a grief I do not know how to carry for you. I am sorry."*
- **(Arden) "Are there others like me?"** → *"Fewer than there once were. More than you believe. Some are hiding. Some have forgotten what they are. You will meet them, in time. They will know you."*
- **"What about the ones hunting us?"** → *"The Legio Null will come. Soon. Your settlement was visible from the sky for one heartbeat. That is enough. You must leave this place. I cannot stop the hunt. I can only tell you to RUN."*
- **Coercion** *(amused)* → *"You are holding a Band you cannot control in a settlement your enemies will find within the week. I am the hand offered to you in the dark. If you prefer another, seek it. I will not chase you."*

## §6 — The Evacuation: The Emptying of Lux Æterna (35–50 min) — MUST HIT

> **Lux Æterna is EVACUATED, NOT DESTROYED.** The canyon survives — emptied. Wards left humming over a hollow town. The Legio Null occupies ground that still stands. The people scatter and live. *(GM-only: this hollow, intact canyon is the seed of Luminas. Do not say so. End on "we will come back," not on fire.)*

### Read-Aloud — Up into the raid
> *You come out of the Warrens into a canyon that is already moving. The three-blast horn is Sabel's evacuation call — he drilled them for this and prayed he'd never use it. Lanterns are being doused, not lit. People carry children and nothing else. And at the canyon's mouth, past the dogleg, the first Sand-Riders of the Legio Null are dismounting in the dark — gear-and-chain on their tabards, crossbows up, an Inquisitor's lantern throwing a cold blue light that makes the shadows lie down flat.*
>
> *Sabel is at the well, voice flat and carrying: "Bolt Hole. Now. Lira's Run. MOVE." He sees you. "You — you're with me at the back. You're what they came for. So you leave LAST." A beat. "Don't make me regret saying that."*

### Structure: a 3-stage chase + 2 fights
A **structured pursuit.** Track one number: the **Pursuit Clock** (starts at 0).
- Each **failed** check, or each round a fight runs long, ticks the Clock **+1.**
- **Triumph** anywhere = clear 1 tick. **Despair** = +1 tick *and* a complication.
- **Clock 3 →** the Sand-Riders catch up: trigger the next fight early / reinforce it.
- **Clock 6 →** a hard cost (see Failure Costs). The escape still succeeds — this is not a TPK — but it bleeds.

Each stage needs **2 party successes** (any PCs) to advance. Civilians are the stakes throughout.

#### STAGE 1 — Cross the open market floor (under crossbows)
Open ground between them and the rim path. The Inquisitor's blue light hunts for mages.
| Approach | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cover-to-cover | Coordination/Athletics ◆◆ | Setback if a PC is openly casting (the blue light flags magic). |
| Keep civilians moving | Leadership/Discipline ◆◆ | Success also protects a named face (Marta, Petra, Selin). |
| Kill the light | Ranged ◆◆◆ (the Inquisitor's lantern) | Triumph: the Sand-Riders lose coordination — clear 2 ticks. |
On 2 successes → the rim path. → **FIGHT 1.**

#### FIGHT 1 — The Dogleg Ambush
A Sand-Rider squad has flanked up the dogleg to cut the rim path.
```
LEGIO NULL SAND-RIDERS — MINION GROUP (start: 4)
Brawn 3 | Agility 3 | Intellect 2 | Cunning 2 | Willpower 2 | Presence 2
Wounds: 5 per minion (group pool = 5 × number in group)
Soak: 4 (legion armor) | Defense: 1
Group skills (only while 2+ remain): Ranged 2, Melee 2, Coordination, Vigilance
  Crossbow — Ranged · Damage 7 · Crit 3 · Medium · Pierce 1
  Legion blade — Melee · Damage 5 · Crit 3 · Engaged
ABILITIES
• Purge Drill — while 2+ remain, add ◼ to the group's combat checks.
• Mage-Shackle (1/encounter) — on a hit vs an openly-casting PC + Despair
  or 3 t: that PC is immobilized until they spend a maneuver + ◆◆ Athletics
  to break free. (Sabel or an ally can free them instead.)
```
**Sabel fights here** — crossbow Damage 7, and a Leadership ◆◆ each round to hand a PC a free maneuver. He is *covering*, not charging. Win = wipe the group or break past (Athletics ◆◆ to shove through while others hold). → **STAGE 2.**

#### STAGE 2 — The climb to the Bolt Hole
The concealed exit is up the south wall. Vertical, dark, under sporadic fire, civilians ahead needing hands.
| Approach | Check | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free climb / haul others up | Athletics ◆◆◆ (◆◆ if roped) | Each success also hauls a civilian to safety. |
| Steady the line under fire | Discipline/Cool ◆◆ | Prevents panic-falls. |
| Rear-guard suppression | Ranged ◆◆ | Buys the climbers ◼; Threat = you're the last one exposed. |
On 2 successes → the Bolt Hole mouth. → **STAGE 3 / FIGHT 2.**

#### STAGE 3 + FIGHT 2 — The Bottleneck & Sabel's Last-Out
The Bolt Hole takes one person at a time. Civilians are still going through. A second Sand-Rider group (start: 3, +1 if Clock ≥3) closes on the mouth. **Someone has to hold the gap.**

> *Sabel plants himself at the mouth of Lira's Run — the dry-riverbed exit named for a child he couldn't save, though none of you know that yet. "I'm last. I'm always last. Get them through." He doesn't say it like a hero. He says it like a man reading a duty roster.*

**The beat — give the players the choice:**
- **Let Sabel hold alone:** he survives (he does NOT die tonight — he's needed), but takes wounds and the Clock jumps +2; the cost lands elsewhere (Failure Costs).
- **Hold WITH him:** run FIGHT 2 (same Sand-Rider block). Clear it or break contact in ≤3 rounds → *everyone* gets out clean. Sabel uses his name for the first time on whichever PC stood with him — that's the reward. (*"...thank you. [Name].")*

When the last civilian and the party are through, Sabel comes last, seals the Bolt Hole, and they drop into the dark of Lira's Run heading south — then arc for the **north road** the Architect named.

### Failure Costs (instead of death — pick by what hurts)
- A named NPC is captured (Selin? Korel?) → a rescue hook later.
- A PC takes a lasting wound / the Mage-Shackle marks them as "seen" by the Legio Null.
- The party loses the supply cache and starts the road north hungry.
- Sabel takes a wound that slows him for the next leg (but lives).

### Read-Aloud — Looking back (the emptying, not the burning)
> *From the lip of Lira's Run you can see the whole canyon below. It is not burning. That's the strange part — you keep waiting for fire and it doesn't come. Lux Æterna is just... empty. The lanterns still glow where no one doused them in time, steady and warm over streets with no one in them. The wards still hum. Marta's pot is still on the cold hearth. The well is still steaming.*
>
> *The Legio Null walks into a town that simply isn't there anymore — the people gone into the desert in a dozen directions, carrying the canyon in their heads instead of on a map. Sabel watches for exactly three breaths. Then he turns north, and so do you.*
>
> *Somewhere behind you, a home you barely had time to have keeps its lights on for no one. It will be there when someone comes back. Someone will come back.*

> **GM (do not read):** That last line is the Luminas seed. The scattered people of Lux Æterna are the foundation stock. Keep this in your pocket for the long game.
