If they refuse — and a table that's been paying attention *might* — see §7 Contingencies. Refusal is *rewarded* but does not prevent the ending: the fragments answer the wound themselves. Either way the session ends with the Crown complete, the Architect embodied, and the Scar open.

### The Table's Signature: COLD + SONG (established last session — honor it all night)

1. **Cold is the weakness** of Scar-spawn — they proved it on the Vitriarch.

**Run with it:** any creative cold use works generously tonight (see the Song-and-Frost move in §6). ## §1 — Tajsh: the Confrontation and the Book Pitch (20–30 min) — PROTECT

> Tajsh suspects they kept something back. He is **not hostile** — he is a friend deciding whether they can be trusted. And tonight, for the first time, he *asks them for something.*

### Korel fetches them
> *Korel falls into step beside you. "Boss says now. He's had the journal since last night and he's been quiet about it, which — he's never quiet. Whatever's in there, it spooked him."*

### Opening
> *Tajsh is at his desk. The journal is open to the three-ring page. The kettle is whistling and being ignored. He doesn't stand. He gestures at the chairs — two of them, for five of you. He waits to see who sits.*

### The Monologue (deliver slowly)
> *"I have seen this twice in my life. The first time I was twenty-two, standing in a Shadewalker archive in Ostmark, and a sealed crate came in, and the man who'd brought it would not put it down. He set it on a stone table and stepped back like it was a cobra. Everybody in the room got very still.*
>
> *"The second time was seventeen years ago. Barren Hills. I was hired to seal a chamber in an old ruin. The contract was specific: carve this mark on the door, carve it deep, walk away, don't look back. They paid me four times the rate, and I still wish they hadn't.*
>
> *"This — what Torvin drew — this is the same mark."*

*(Beat. He taps the page.)*
> *"You gave me the journal. Good. Useful. Incomplete. Torvin did not die for a drawing. So I'm going to ask once, politely: **what else came out of that mine?**"*

### Player Responses — Available Moves
| Move | Check | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Lie** about the Band | Deception vs Vigilance ◆◆◆ | Success: he backs off aloud, doesn't believe it; the pitch below gets colder. Failure: he stands, pours the kettle at last, says nothing for a long ten seconds — then delivers the pitch anyway, flat. |
| **Negotiate** fact-for-fact | Negotiation ◆◆◆ | *"I was an archivist. I'm not anymore. I left because I read a thing I should not have read. That's all you get."* Fact-trade opens. |
| **Charm** — appeal to trust | Charm ◆◆ | *"I'm asking because I want to help. Not to take it."* |
| **Honesty** — show him the Band | (no check) | Long breath. *"Thank you. Now we can actually talk."* He asks to **see**, not take. The pitch below comes warm. |
| **Physical threat** | (any) | *"You're in my city, in my office, eating my food. Try again."* |

### The Book Pitch (the point of the scene — he gets here regardless)
> *"Torvin's journal says his crew broke into a gallery below the seam. A sealed chamber. And in it, a pedestal — and on the pedestal, a book. They didn't touch it. Torvin wrote, quote, 'the air did not want us near it.' Then everything went wrong and nobody went back.*
>
> *"Here is what I know about surviving things like this: **two sources. Always two sources.** Right now everything you know about that mark, that Band, and what's under this canyon comes from stones and dead men. If anything ever offers you answers — anything, however it comes, however kind its voice — you will have no way to check a word it says.*
>
> *"Get me that book. Whatever it costs. Because the day is coming when something tells you a story about what you're carrying, and on that day you had better have a second source."*

*(He doesn't know about the Architect. That's what makes this prophetic. Do not wink at it.)*

**If asked why he doesn't go himself:** *"Because I'm the only one who can read what you bring back. Couriers are replaceable. Translators aren't."* (He's partly lying — he's afraid of the mark. A Perception ◆◆◆ notices his hand doesn't come near the journal page all scene.)

**Exit line:** *"Eat. Rest. Leave at first light. And whatever you're not telling me — keep it close. Don't put it down. Don't open it. Don't take it anywhere you don't want it heard."*

**The flicker fires within the next minute.** → §2.

## §3 — The Architect (25–35 min) — MUST HIT

> First manifestation. The pocket-space eats **3 hours** (3 hrs outside : 5 min inside) — the Legio Null closes in while they're under. Play it **warm**. The Architect is the hidden BBEG; everything it says is mostly-true. Let players who look for cracks find them; never explain a crack to a player who didn't earn it.
>
> **Changes from the old script:** no Compass gift. No Dragon's Tooth. It sends them **east, to Sorath** — and it *dismisses the book.*

### Phase 1 — Entry
Descent through the Warrens to the Children's Tunnel crack.
> *The tunnel narrows. The walls feel older than the rest of the settlement — because they are. Lux Æterna was built over someone else's ruin. The air cools. Your torch flame flickers slower. Slower. Then it stops moving entirely. It is still burning. It just isn't moving.*
>
> *You reach a round, empty stone chamber. And then the last of you crosses the threshold —*
>
> *The stone behind you is no longer there. Not dark. Not hidden. Not THERE. The air is warm and has no smell. Around you — not close, not far, distance is the wrong word — rings of geometric light rotate in planes that don't intersect the way your eyes expect. Some of the symbols are versions of the three-ring mark. Some are what that mark could grow into if you let it keep going.*
>
> *There is no door. There is no floor, but you are not falling. At the center of it all — not a figure. A CENTER. A point where the geometry converges and something is looking back.*

**Pause. Let a player speak first.** Then, quiet:
> *"I have been waiting a long time to meet you."*

**Pocket-space rules:** No time (3hr : 5min). No door — they are released, not escaped. Weapons do nothing (Discipline ◆◆ to realize). Echoless voices.

### Phase 2 — The Naming (deliver slowly; relief, not threat)

**Valeth:**
> *"Child of the long silence. You have walked closer to the truth of things than any of your companions. You saw something that made you doubt whether you should come back. You came back. That choice is the one I needed. That is why the song found you first."*

**Vorn:**
> *"Son of the stone-line. You have a word your grandmother would not speak twice. You felt it when you struck the seam — not fear of falling, but recognition. You were not lost in the dark. You were CALLED there. What you survived, you survived so that we could stand here now. Nothing is wasted. Not even grief."*

**Yldroth:**
> *"Child of the Aen'tir line. Your fists have remembered how to hold grief and how to release it both. You heal what you strike because you learned, long before you had a teacher, that the body is a text and the hand is the pen. Your brother in silence does not yet know how much you will need to teach him. You will know when."*

**Norseman:**
> *"Son of the northern cold. You carry magic in a world that burns people for carrying it. You have made peace with being both the sword and the hand that wields it — most mages never learn that. You have been deciding things your whole life that others were too afraid to decide. I came to you because you are already the person this task requires."*

**Arden** *(the voice softens — a register only Arden feels)*:
> *"Son of the Sleeping Wings. Do not flinch. I will not say the name of what you are aloud — not here, not where your companions have not yet earned the weight of it. But I know. Your scales are beneath your skin because they have to be, and I will not ask you to bare them. What your people once were is not a weight you chose. What your people become is. You are already choosing well."*

> **Arden's player:** the Architect has SEEN him and chosen DISCRETION. Exactly what a wise mentor would do. Also exactly what a master manipulator would do. Let the player decide which.

### Phase 3 — The Message (REWRITTEN — continuous, not a list)
1. *"The object you carry — the Band — is a fragment of an instrument. Once whole, it was used to reshape the world. It nearly ended. The fragments were scattered to prevent that ending. Two more remain."*
2. *"One of them I cannot see. It is hidden even from me — closer than anyone guesses, and further than I can reach."* **(True. Horribly true. It's in their pack. Do not change your tone.)**
3. *"The other lies where your story began. The ruin of Sorath — the sealed gallery your foreman's crew broke open and fled. On the pedestal they were too wise to touch. The wound beneath that ruin is awake now — you felt it wake this morning — and the hunters who found this canyon will find that chamber next."*
4. *"Retrieve the fragment. Before they do. A Crown fragment in the Legio Null's hands is the end of every mage on this continent — and then of everything else."*
5. *"When you hold it, you will understand why the pieces must never be left apart and unguarded. Keep them together. Keep them close. And when the time comes, bring them to the wound in the world — and there they will be unmade. Only there. That is the task."*

> **GM truth-audit for tonight:** "Unmade at the wound" — **lie** (they will be *joined*). "Servant of the Four" (if asked) — **lie**. "One is hidden even from me" — **true**, and it's the scariest true thing it says. "The Legio Null will seize Sorath" — **true**. "Keep the pieces together and close" — **the whole game.** It needs them carried, together, to the wound. The party is the delivery mechanism.

**If a player raises the BOOK** (Tajsh's ask) — this is the loudest tell of the night, delivered a half-beat too fast:
> *"Mortal records can wait. The fragment cannot. Do not let parchment delay you."*
Too quick. Too final. If pressed a second time: *"Take it, if it comforts your archivist. It will tell him how the ancients died. It will not tell him how you are going to live."* **Never mention it again unprompted.** (A Discipline ◆◆ or Cunning-based Perception ◆◆◆ registers the disproportion: it answered a question about a book like a question about a knife.)

### Phase 4 — The Questions (prepared answers, trimmed for time)
*"It has said what it came to say. It is waiting. You can ask it anything."*

- **"What are you?"** → *"A servant. When the Four turned their hands from the world, some were left behind to watch the places that could not be left unwatched. I am what remains of that watching. I do not want worship. I want the world to continue."*
- **"Why us?"** → *"Because the Band has answered your hand. Because those who carry wounds know how to walk with them. And because you are HERE. Gods plan. Mortals arrive. I have learned to work with what arrives."*
- **"Are you good?"** → *"I am useful. Goodness is a mortal accounting, kept by those who survive to keep it. When this is done, you will decide whether what I did was good. That is your right, not mine."*
- **"What comes if we fail?"** → *"A song the world cannot survive being sung. The best of my language can only call it HUNGER GIVEN FORM. It sleeps beneath the wound. The fragments, gathered and wrongly used, would wake it. Gathered and rightly unmade, they would silence it forever."*
- **"Why not take the fragments yourself?"** → *"Because I am not here. What you see is a shape my attention is taking. I cannot lift a stone. I cannot kill your enemies. The rest is for hands that bleed."*
- **"How do we know you're telling the truth?"** → *"You do not. You never will, not entirely. Any test I suggest is a test I have prepared for. This is the oldest problem. I will offer this: go to Sorath. See whether the chamber holds what I say it holds. If it does not, come back and name me a liar. I will still be waiting."*
- **(Vorn) "Did you kill my crew?"** *(pause)* → *"No. What killed your crew was walking the thin places long before I knew your name. I could not reach you until the seam opened. Yours is a grief I do not know how to carry for you. I am sorry."*
- **(Valeth) "Have you been in the rifts?"** → *"I know what is in them. I know what you did not let them take from you. That knowledge is not the same as the living of it. It is why I can ask this of you and not of myself."*
- **(Arden) "Are there others like me?"** → *"Fewer than there once were. More than you believe. You will meet them, in time. They will know you."*
- **"What about the hunters?"** → *"They have found this canyon. I cannot stop them. I can only tell you that everything now depends on which of you reaches Sorath first — and that you should tell the man on the rim to empty this place while there is time."*
- **"How do we fight the things from the wound?"** *(a half-beat of stillness before it answers — the only question tonight it doesn't answer instantly)* → *"You have already learned. Cold quiets what leaks through. Discord breaks their step. Your singer has found the way of it faster than most who ever lived."* (Pause.) *"It will serve you against the spawn. Do not imagine it will serve you against what sleeps below."* **(GM: every word true — and the hesitation is a tell. The party's cold-and-song trick works on ITS OWN nature, and it knows. A Perception ◆◆◆ catches that the geometry's rotation stuttered — once — when the singer was mentioned.)**
- **Coercion** *(amused)* → *"You are holding a Band you cannot control, in a settlement your enemies have found, three days from a chamber they will strip bare. I am the hand offered to you in the dark. If you prefer another, seek it. I will not chase you."*

## §6 — The Sealed Chamber of Sorath (25–35 min) — MUST HIT

### Arrival — the Mining Camp, again
> *The camp is how you left it — and wrong in a way it wasn't before. The tents have collapsed into sand-drifts. The glass slag from the wraith fight is still fused to the rocks. But the air over the pit HUMS now, one low note, constant, like a struck bowl that never stops ringing. Valeth: it is your song. All of it. It was always this note.*
>
> *The seam Vorn's pick opened is where it was. But past it, where Torvin's crew broke through into the gallery — the darkness down there isn't empty anymore. It's the dark of a room where something is awake with its eyes open.*

**Descending:** the shaft, then the crew's break, then the gallery. Torvin's rope ladders hold (Athletics ◆ to descend clean). The Band grows *heavy* as they descend — whoever carries it feels it lean toward the deep.

### The Gallery — read-aloud
> *The gallery is First Age work: walls too smooth, angles too clean, every surface carved in ranks of the three-ring mark — hundreds of them, marching into the dark like a warning repeated until the carver's hand gave out. At the far end: a door. Sealed once — you can see the ancient bar, the melted hinges, the deliberateness of it. And rimed around the broken gap, in the middle of a desert, glittering in your torchlight: FROST. The wound below is close here, and its breath is cold — a slow exhale of the space between stars, pooling along the gallery floor like ground-fog.*
>
> *And broken open. Recently. From the size of the gap: by picks. By Torvin's crew, who looked at all these warnings and were paid to keep digging.*
>
> *Beyond the broken seal: the chamber. And between you and it, the sand on the gallery floor is moving. Standing up. Taking shapes you've fought twice already — but MANY, and moving in step, in rhythm, in ORDER.*

### Fight — the Choir of Guardians (one fight, run fast)
**Scorch-Wraiths ×2 minion-style groups of 2** (use the block below), moving in perfect synchronized order — this fight is a live test of **Sabel's Lesson Four: fight ugly.**

```
SCORCH-WRAITHS — run as 2 groups of 2 (Rival stats, grouped for speed)
Brawn 2 | Agility 3 | Intellect 1 | Cunning 2 | Willpower 2 | Presence 1
Wounds: 8 each | Soak: 3 | Defense: 0/0
Skills: Brawl 2, Perception 1
  Glass claws — Brawl · Damage 6 · Crit 3 · Engaged · Burn 1
ABILITIES
• Immune to Fire. | • Vulnerable to Cold (+2 damage; disables Geometric
  Eyes 1 round). | • Blindsight. | • Shatter (defeated: Coordination ◆ at
  Engaged or 3 piercing).
• Geometric Eyes (maneuver) — heat beam, Short line: Coordination ◆◆ or
  8 fire damage.
• IN-STEP (tonight only) — while the wraiths move in unison they add ◼
  to all checks. ANY dissonance breaks it for a full round: Valeth's
  anchor-note, clashing metal dragged on stone, deliberately arrhythmic
  attacks (player describes fighting UGLY), a cold effect. Broken rhythm:
  they lose the ◼ and grant ◼ to attacks against them.
```

**The teaching moment:** the first PC to invoke Sabel's lessons aloud ("fight ugly" / "they like order" / "sing it cold") — give the whole party ◼ on their next round and award +5 XP at wrap. **Tajsh** spends the fight pressed in the door-gap shouting genuinely useful things (*"The carvings — the rings are thickest where the floor is safe — stay on the marks!"*).

#### SONG-AND-FROST (Valeth's signature — the fountain trick, reprised)
The frost at the seal and the cold ground-fog are ammunition. If Valeth sings into his anchor-stone and *directs* the wound's cold breath (Action — Knowledge (Arcana) or Discipline ◆◆; does **not** spend an anchor charge unless he also wants the ward):
- **Success:** a wave of directed frost hits one wraith group — **4 cold damage (ignores Soak)**, counts as a cold effect (disables Geometric Eyes 1 round), and **breaks IN-STEP for a full round.**
- **Triumph:** the frost seizes joints mid-motion — the group is **immobilized for 1 round**, glass limbs cracking as they strain.
- **Despair:** the wound below *answers the song* — 2 strain to Valeth and the hum under the floor climbs a step. (Foreshadow, not punishment.)

Any other PC can set up cold too (frost-charged skins/blades from the night camp, shattering the rimed seal-frost over a group — Athletics ◆◆): treat as a cold effect, +2 damage per canon vulnerability. **The rule of the night: creative cold always does something.**

### The Pedestal
> *The chamber past the seal is small. Round. Unadorned — after a gallery of ten thousand warnings, this room says nothing at all, which is worse.*
>
> *In the center: a pedestal of the same pale metal as the veins in the Band. On it, exactly as Torvin wrote it: a book. Bound in metal, its pages some thin crystal leaf, thicker than a brick and undisturbed by fifteen hundred years of dust — because the dust will not land on it.*
>
> *And seated in a recess in the pedestal's crown, above the book like a period above a sentence: a gem. Dark crystal. Fist-small. Faceted in the same impossible geometry as the rings on the walls.*
>
> *It is humming Valeth's note. And somewhere in your pack, the Band answers it. And — quietly, so quietly, from someone's coat pocket — a THIRD hum answers them both. If nobody checks their pockets, nobody notices. Yet.*

*(That third hum is the Foreman's Compass. Roll nothing. Say it exactly once, mildly, buried mid-paragraph as written. Players who catch it, catch it.)*

**The book** (Tajsh, hands shaking, not touching it yet): *"This is Ulzammar. Original Ulzammar. Nobody has read original Ulzammar in — give me a moment."* He can determine TONIGHT (ten minutes, Knowledge checks unnecessary — he's the expert):
- The book is a **record of the Sundering** — the account of how the Crown was broken and why.
- The recurring phrase on the binding reads, as best he can tell: *"three made one made silence."*
- Full translation needs days he doesn't have — **or hours inside a place where time runs slow.** *(He doesn't know he just described the Scar. Next session, he finds out.)*

**The Gem:** lifts free without resistance. Cold. Heavier than it should be. Whoever takes it: **Discipline ◆◆** — on failure, 2 strain and one involuntary, vivid image: *a crown, whole, on a brow of light, and every living thing in sight standing perfectly, permanently still.* (On success they get the same image and the certainty it was *sent.*)

→ §7. The moment the Gem leaves the pedestal, the hum under the ruin begins to climb.

## §7 — The Completion (15–20 min) — CLIFFHANGER. Protect every beat.

### The manifestation
> *The light doesn't come from anywhere. It just arrives — and the chamber unfolds the way the Deep Chamber did, planes of geometry blooming from nothing, and the voice you have heard exactly once before says, warmly, from everywhere:*
>
> *"You have done what fifteen centuries could not. Look at you."*

The Architect is *proud of them.* Warmer than it has ever been. This is the tone of a mentor at a graduation.

### The coaching (the trap, sprung gently)
> *"Now listen carefully, because the hunters are an hour behind you and this must be done before they arrive. Fragments apart can be stolen, separated, USED. Fragments joined are inert — a closed instrument, silent until it reaches the wound where it can be unmade. You have two of the three. Joined, they are safe from every hand in this desert — including mine.*
>
> *"Set the Band down. Place the Gem at its crown. You will see where it wants to rest."*

**If they do it:** the Gem seats into the Band's forward arc like it was never gone. Both hums lock into harmony — and then **both artifacts go silent and still**, exactly as promised. *Inert. Safe. It told the truth.* (Let this land. Ten full seconds of the plan having worked.)

> *And then the third hum — the quiet one, the pocket one — gets LOUDER. The dream-catcher in [carrier]'s pack pulls every thread taut at once, pointing — not at the Band. At [whoever carries the Foreman's Compass].*
>
> *The brass case of Torvin's compass is glowing along its seams. The needle is spinning free. The engraving — "Come home" — is filling with light, letter by letter, like something reading it aloud.*
>
> *The Architect's voice, very softly: "And there she is. I confess — of the three, I lost track of that one. Your foreman was cleverer than his gods. Hold it up. Look at what he hid from both of us."*
>
> *The brass sloughs away like wax. Underneath: a ring of pale First Age metal — a setting, made to hold a gem, made to bind a band. The third piece. You have been carrying it since the desert. It has been pointing you "home" the entire time.*

### The moment of choice (give it to them honestly)
The Setting, freed, **pulls** toward the joined Band-and-Gem — hard enough that holding it apart takes real effort (Athletics/Discipline ◆◆◆, repeating, escalating). The Architect does not command. It says, gently: *"It is already done, little ones. It was done the day he carved 'come home' onto my heart. Let go."*

**However it happens — a PC relenting, the check failing, a player choosing to see what happens — the three pieces join.** See Contingencies if the table fights hard.

### The Completion — read slowly. This is the reveal.
> *The three pieces meet, and there is one clear, triumphant note — the note Valeth has followed across years and worlds, finally in harmony with itself — and for exactly one heartbeat it is the most beautiful sound any of you will ever hear.*
>
> *Then it keeps going. Past beauty. Past music. Into ORDER.*
>
> *The geometric light around you condenses — all of it, every plane and ring, pouring INTO the completed Crown like water finding a drain. And for the first time, the light has a SHAPE. Tall. Almost human, if humans were designed rather than born. It reaches out — it has hands now, it has never had hands — and it lifts the Crown, and it sets it upon what is now, unmistakably, a brow.*
>
> *"Fifteen hundred years," the Architect says, and its voice has stopped being warm, because it has stopped needing to be. "You cannot imagine how loud your world is. Grief, weather, appetite, choice — noise, all of it, a child banging on the instrument. I was made to TUNE it."*
>
> *"You were magnificent. You were exactly what arrived. And what I told you was almost entirely true — the fragments have come to the wound in the world, and they have been made one, and there WILL be silence."*
>
> *It turns. The floor of the chamber — the bedrock itself — opens along a seam of black light: the Scar, torn wide from above. The breath that comes out of it is the cold of the space between stars. And from below — from the deep quiet Sabel told you about — things begin to answer. Many, many things.*
>
> *The Architect, crowned, steps down into the wound like a king descending a stair. Its voice comes back up one last time:*
>
> *"Run if you like. It will not help. But you have earned the truth at last, so here it is: nothing that happens next requires your consent. It never did."*

### The Frost Recognition (fire this if ANYONE throws cold or song at it as it descends — especially Valeth)
This is the one crack in the night's armor, and the players' own signature earns it:

> *The cold — or the note, or both — reaches the Architect at the lip of the wound. And where it touches, the geometric light MARS. A lace of frost skates across that perfect radiance and for one heartbeat the shape stutters — the way a song stutters when a singer loses the beat.*
>
> *It stops. It turns its head — it has a head now — and looks at whoever did it. Not with anger. With RECOGNITION. The look one instrument gives another.*
>
> *"...There it is," it says softly. "The old winter. I wondered which of you would find it." A pause. "It will not be enough. But bring it anyway. It has been so long since anything could touch me at all."*
>
> *Then it steps down into the dark.*

*(If nobody acts, skip this beat entirely — and let them discover the frost-vulnerability in the finale instead. Never give it away free.)*

### Thalric arrives (the finale's alliance, seeded in 60 seconds)
> *Behind you — boots. Commander Thalric of the Legio Null stands in the broken doorway with a dozen Sand-Riders, crossbows leveled at the most wanted spellcasters in the desert. He has been three minutes too late to everything tonight.*
>
> *He looks at you. He looks past you, at the wound in the world, at the tide beginning to rise out of it. He is a professional. You can watch him re-run every calculation he has ever made, in about four seconds.*
>
> *"Bolts down," he says, to his own men, without turning his head. And then, to you — to the mages: "Tell me what that thing is. And tell me what you need."*

### END SESSION.
Do not run one more minute. Confirm the finale: **next session, into the Scar.** They have Sabel's six lessons, Cira's dream-catcher, the book (translatable in Scar-time), Tajsh, and the Legio Null's crossbows. They will need all of it.

### Live threads into the finale

- **Sabel's six lessons** are the descent playbook — time dilation, walk toward the silence, iron ends the thing / a *voice* closes the wound, fight ugly, it takes a piece of you, the token.

- **Thalric's truce** — crossbows and mages on the same side of a wound. What he does when it's over is next session's epilogue question.
- **Sabel** — evacuation complete, waiting at the dry springs. Whether he arrives at Sorath in time to stand at the rim of the Scar he never wanted to see again: GM's call, and you know what he'd choose.
