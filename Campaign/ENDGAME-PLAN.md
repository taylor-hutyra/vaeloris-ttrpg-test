# Endgame Plan — *Three Made One Made Silence*

> **How to use this doc:** after every session, tick the beats you actually reached and jot one line in the Progress Log (§8). The spine (§2) is written as **beats, not sessions** — the table plays slow, so any "session" below may take two real evenings. Suggested break points are marked ⏸. When you're one session away from a beat, ask Claude to expand it into a full table runsheet (like SESSION-04-Runsheet.md).

## §1 — Where We Are (campaign state at time of writing)

**Played through:** town exploration; the Vitriarch fight at the well (won with cold + Valeth's song-directed frozen fountain — now the table's signature); Sabel's Scar story (six lessons; cold magic killed his; a warder's voice closed the seam).

**Prepped, not yet played:** [Session-04/SESSION-04-Runsheet.md](Session-04/SESSION-04-Runsheet.md) — Tajsh's book pitch → the Architect (sends them east) → send-off (canyon evacuates empty) → ride to Sorath → sealed chamber (book + Gem) → **the Completion**: Foreman's Compass revealed as the Setting, Crown completed, Architect embodied, Scar torn open, Thalric's "Bolts down" truce. 

**The pivot in one line:** the Architect is crowned and has descended into the open Sorath Scar to take the wound's deepest point. The party must go down and end it.

**The confirmed weapon:** **the sundering-chord (from the pedestal book), sung cold** — Valeth's voice + directed frost, everything the table already proved on the Vitriarch and Sabel confirmed from his own history.

**Real-world constraint:** ~2 sessions before the move, table plays slow. §6 has the compression levers. The story must be able to LAND.

### BEAT 1 — The Rim (aftermath + alliance; ~30–45 table min)
The night at the top of the wound. Load-bearing scenes:
- [ ] **Thalric's terms.** He holds the rim with crossbows and discipline; the mages go down. Nobody says the word "pardon." His line to hang the truce on: *"I hunt what magic does to the world. Tonight I watched what the world does without it. Go. I'll keep the door."*
- [ ] **The spawn tide.** Lesser fiends leak upward in pulses. The Legio Null's ordered volleys FEED them (Sabel's lesson four, proven at scale) — a PC has to teach Thalric's men to fight ugly. The mage-hunters taking tactical orders from mages: let it be uncomfortable, then let it work.
- [ ] **Sabel arrives.** From the dry springs, having seen the light on the horizon. He looks at the open Scar — the thing he's spent seventeen years not talking about — and starts checking everyone's water. If the table earned it, this is where he hands over the token (the notched iron spike / his crewmate's tags): *"Bring it back or don't come back confused about which matters."*
- [ ] **Tajsh's partial read, topside:** the book confirms the Crown was sundered ONCE before — by an Ulzammar choir singing the world's own noise back into it. Full chord needs more time than the surface has. *"The translation needs a week. So we had better go somewhere a week fits in an afternoon."* (He now knows what the Scar's time-dilation is FOR.)
- [ ] **Cira's dream-catcher** re-tunes: threads now point DOWN. Constantly. It has become a depth gauge for wrongness.

⏸ *Clean break point if Session 4 spilled over — "next session: down."*

### BEAT 2 — The Descent (Sabel's lessons as level design; ~60–90 table min)
The Scar interior, structured as the six lessons paying off in order:
- [ ] **Lesson 1 — Time lies:** mark the way in (player ritual — let them decide HOW: chalk, rope, Vorn's stone-sense, blood). Establish the dilation working FOR them: Tajsh translates as they walk. Hours inside; the surface holds its breath for days.
- [ ] **Lesson 4 — Fight ugly:** one herding encounter — lesser fiends pushing them off-course (they don't want a fight; they want to STEER them away from the silence). Winning = realizing they're being herded and refusing. Cold + dissonance carves the path.
- [ ] **Lesson 2 — Walk toward the silence:** navigation set-piece, not combat. The Scar is a maze of noise (echoes of other places, other times, leaked memories — *this is where you can show anything from the campaign's past*). The path to the Architect is wherever the noise DIES. Cira's dream-catcher + Vorn's "recognition" (his Naming: he was CALLED once; he knows this pull) lead the way.
- [ ] **Valeth's ghost-beat (optional, powerful):** traces of his lost crew — a Song-Ward casing, a cold campsite years-stale, a name carved somewhere no one alive should have reached. Closure available, not forced. If the table is slow, this is the first cut — but it's the best 10 minutes of quiet in the finale.
- [ ] **Lesson 5 — It takes a piece of you:** the toll. Crossing the last threshold into the deep quiet costs each PC something SMALL and SPECIFIC (a memory of a smell, the sound of a parent's voice, the ability to ever again hear a specific note). Let each player choose their own; write them down; they're epilogue material. Tajsh pays too: he loses the memory of reading his first book. He keeps translating anyway.
- [ ] **The translation completes** at the threshold: the sundering-chord is three notes — *the Measure, the Setting, the Stone* — sung not in harmony but DELIBERATELY OFF, the world's noise aimed at the Crown's perfect order. (Vorn's grandmother's rhyme was the chord's names all along. "Do not speak the fourth" — the fourth note is the Crown's own, the one that must never be joined.)

⏸ *Strong break point: at the threshold of the deep quiet, chord in hand, before the boss. If the move deadline allows a third session, THIS is where it splits.*

### BEAT 3 — The Deep Quiet (the boss; ~60–75 table min)
- [ ] **The arena:** the old Anchoring Fiend's hollow — and the old Anchor is DEAD, absorbed mid-struggle into a wall of ordered glass. The Architect didn't join the Scar's ecosystem; it ATE the thing holding the wound open and took the job. (One look at this tells the party the Scar-spawn were never its allies — nothing is. Order has no allies, only components.)
- [ ] **The fight** — see §3 boss design. Three movements: the Still Field, the Breaking, the Sundering.
- [ ] **The chord lands.** The Crown comes apart. What's left of the Architect — see §3 for the two ending flavors — goes silent.
- [ ] **Lesson 3, second half — a voice closes the wound:** the fight ends but the Scar is still open and now ANCHORLESS — collapsing inward, hungry. Valeth sings the seam shut from the inside while the party holds the exit path (final skill-challenge sprint, the "marked way in" from Beat 2 paying off literally). If his anchor-stone has one charge left, it shatters here, on the last note. It should.

### BEAT 4 — The Surface & Epilogues (~30–45 table min; NEVER cut)
- [ ] **Re-emergence + the time question.** How long were they down? Choose at the table by what the story needs: days (clean), or a full season (autumn when they left; spring when they climb out — the world went on; the emotional cost of Scar-time made real). Recommended: **a season.**
- [ ] **What they find:** Thalric held the rim as long as anything came out; the Legio Null camp is now half field-hospital, half memorial. Sabel is still there. He was always going to still be there.
- [ ] **The fragments:** unmade / inert / scattered — GM's choice, but they should END. No sequel hooks unless the table begs.
- [ ] **Epilogue round** — §5 menu, one scene per PC + the three NPC closers + Lux Æterna's lights.

## §3 — Boss Design Sketch: The Crowned Architect

*(Expand to full stat block in the finale runsheet — this is the shape.)*

**Concept:** not a brawl — a **structure the party dismantles while it tries to finish tuning them out of existence.** Three movements:

| Movement | What's happening | How it ends |
|---|---|---|
| **I. The Still Field** | Its order-aura is absolute: weapons slow mid-swing, spells resolve a half-beat late, ranks of glass-spawn move IN-STEP. The Architect doesn't attack — it CONDUCTS, and the field itself smothers (strain pressure each round; the "still world" vision from the Gem, happening in real time). | **Cold + dissonance crack the field** — Song-and-Frost, frost-charged iron, deliberately broken rhythm. Each ugly act mars the field locally (the Frost Recognition writ large). Enough cracks → Movement II. |
| **II. The Breaking** | The field is marred; now it fights — reshaping the hollow's geometry, conscripting glass-spawn, closing time around isolated PCs (a PC "paused" like the moth until allies break them out). It targets VALETH above all — it knows what the voice is for. | Party protects the singer and lands **cold iron on the Crown itself** (the only thing that wounds it — Sabel's token spike is narratively perfect here). Crown integrity broken → Movement III opens. |
| **III. The Sundering** | Valeth sings the three-note chord — **one note per round, three rounds, each note requiring a different PC's help** (suggested: Yldroth steadies him when he falters — *"you will know when"*; Vorn names the notes from the rhyme; anyone delivers the cold on the third). The Architect throws everything at breaking the song. | Third note lands cold → the Crown sunders. Fragments fall separate and DEAD. The body of light loses its shape. |

**The two ending flavors (choose at the table):**
- **Silence:** it simply stops — unmade, no last words. The most on-theme.
- **The last question (recommended):** for one heartbeat, the geometry becomes something almost mortal-shaped and small, and it asks — genuinely, without malice — *"Was the noise worth it?"* Nobody has to answer. Then it's gone. Give the table the silence to sit in.

**Failure valves (it's the finale — bend, don't TPK):** if the chord fails a round, the note isn't lost — the round is; the field re-smothers and they must crack it again (cost: wounds, strain, one more "piece of you"). If Valeth drops, Tajsh has the chord phonetically — *"I read it. I can't sing. Somebody hum with me."* Ugly, off-key, mortal — which is, of course, exactly the point. The chord sung badly by five voices works BETTER than sung perfectly by one.

## §5 — Epilogue Menu (Beat 4; pick what the table responds to)

Run as: one narrated closer per PC (player co-authors), then the wide shot. Candidates:

1. **The relighting.** The party walks into empty Lux Æterna and lights one lantern. By the third, people are coming out of the desert. (Strongest final image; ends on the Luminas seed without naming it.)
2. **The pieces-of-you.** Each PC's Scar-toll, revisited one year on — what grew back strange, what never did. Ties the cost off with dignity.
3. **Thalric's report.** Read aloud, dry and factual, over images of the aftermath — the driest possible voice saying the least deniable things about mages. The Decree's first crack.
4. **The Warder's grave.** Sabel travels to the Varnathi Highlands with the party, to a hill village, to tell a seventeen-years-late story to a stone. Says the name out loud. (If the table never learned about Lira — this is where he says that name too.)
5. **Valeth's quiet.** For the first time in years — no song. He gets to decide if the silence is loss or peace. Yldroth sits with him either way.
6. **The empty pedestal.** The sealed chamber, re-sealed by Tajsh — this time with the mark carved by someone who knows exactly what it means. The book goes back. Somebody had better warn the NEXT crew of miners. (Wry closer; use only if the table likes a wink.)

## §6 — Compression Levers (the move is coming; the story must land)

**If everything must fit in 2 sessions** (Session 4 as prepped + ONE finale):
- Fold Beat 1 to 10 minutes: Thalric's terms in one exchange, Sabel arrives DURING it, Tajsh's topside read becomes one line ("the chord is in here — I need Scar-time to find it").
- Beat 2 becomes one montage roll per lesson (three rolls: mark the way / refuse the herding / walk to the silence) + the toll. Cut Valeth's ghost-beat (mourn it, cut it).
- Boss runs all three movements but Movement I cracks on the FIRST good cold/song act.
- Epilogues: the relighting + one line per PC. Non-negotiable even at max compression.

**If a third session materializes:** split at the ⏸ in Beat 2 (threshold of the deep quiet). Session 5 = Rim + Descent (the character session); Session 6 = boss + epilogues (the catharsis session). This is the best-case shape.

**If Session 4 itself runs slow** (likely — the table is slow and §1–§3 of the runsheet are talky): break after the send-off; next session opens on the ride east with the cold tell. The Completion cliffhanger is worth protecting at any scheduling cost — do NOT end a session mid-§6/§7 of the runsheet.

**If the campaign ends abruptly** (move happens, table never reconvenes): the one-scene finale is Beat 3 Movement III run as pure narration + dice-less chord with each player narrating their note's help, then epilogue 1. Thirty minutes, over a video call if it comes to that. The story still lands.
